The gap

The office work that eats the owner's week

In most service businesses, the most expensive hours belong to the owner, and too many of them go to repetitive office work a system could do.

  • Payroll math. Cross-referencing jobs, commissions and bonuses every pay period.
  • Review requests. Asking for reviews only when someone remembers.
  • Onboarding and offboarding. The same emails and steps typed out for every new or leaving client.
  • Problems found too late. Low inventory, missed deadlines and odd numbers spotted after they cost money.

If a task follows the same rules every time, it can usually be automated.

Common questions

Business automation FAQ

What should we automate first?

Usually the task that takes the most time and follows the clearest rules. For most service businesses, that's payroll or review requests.

Do we need to switch software?

No. We build on the CRM, email and Google tools you already use.

What if our rules are complicated?

Complicated is fine as long as it's consistent: split commissions, tiered bonuses and weekly guarantees can all be automated.

What happens if something changes?

Rule changes are updates, not rebuilds. On a retainer, we handle them for you.

What does it cost?

A single automation is a fixed-quote project. Ongoing systems work runs on a monthly retainer.
