The gap

Why most home-service ad accounts waste money

Most owners we talk to aren't running a bad business. They're running an ad account that reports good numbers that aren't true. Google's dashboard says leads are up, but the phone isn't ringing more.

  • Ghost conversions. Map taps, direction clicks and page views counted as "leads." In one audit, nearly 40% of reported conversions were actions like these, not customers.
  • Junk search terms. Keywords that haven't produced a call in a year, and searches for jobs you don't do, parts, DIY help or jobs at competitors.
  • Wrong geography. Ads shown to people outside the area you can actually serve.
  • Budget spread thin. Money split evenly across every service instead of weighted toward the jobs that make you the most profit.

None of that shows up in Google's optimization score. It shows up in what each real customer costs you.

Common questions

Google Ads management FAQ

What is a "ghost conversion"?

An action Google counts as a conversion that isn't a real lead, such as a map tap, a direction click or a page view. Counting them inflates your results and teaches Google's bidding to chase the wrong people.

What does the $1,200 audit include?

A full review of conversion tracking, search terms, keywords, geographic targeting, bidding and budgets, plus a written list of what to fix, ranked by impact. You keep it whether or not we manage the account afterward.

Do you need access to our account?

Yes. You add us as a user on your Google Ads account. You keep ownership of the account and all its history.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

The audit is a one-time project. Ongoing management is a monthly retainer, scoped on a call.

Which industries do you run ads for?

Home-service businesses: appliance repair, roofing, septic and similar trades, plus real estate agents. We know how these businesses get booked, which is why we track calls rather than clicks.

Can you tie ads to the jobs we actually book?

Often, yes. If you use Housecall Pro or JobNimbus, we can connect ad performance to your job data so you see what a booked job really costs.
