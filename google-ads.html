Google Ads without the wasted spend

Full account ownership: tracking that tells the truth, campaigns built around your best jobs, and reporting an owner can actually read.

Most service-business ad accounts leak money in ways the owner never sees: conversion goals that count accidental taps as leads, keywords that haven't produced a call in a year, and geo settings showing ads to people who will never book. The first thing Knoxline does is audit — and the audit almost always pays for itself.

From there we rebuild conversion tracking so a "lead" means a real phone call from an ad, restructure campaigns around your most profitable job types, and manage budgets weekly. Every month you get a plain-English snapshot: calls, cost per call, and where the month is heading. No dashboards you need a translator for.
