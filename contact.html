Do you only work with businesses in Knoxville?

No — Knoxville, TN is home base, but we build websites, manage Google Ads, and automate operations for home-service businesses across the Southeast. Current clients are in Tennessee, Alabama, Georgia, and Texas, and everything runs remote.

What does working with Knoxline look like?

Monthly retainers: your website, SEO, Google Business Profile, Google Ads, dashboards, and automations built, managed, and improved continuously — one partner for the whole system instead of a patchwork of vendors.

What CRMs and tools do you work with?

Whatever your business already runs — JobNimbus, Housecall Pro, Shopify, Klaviyo, GoHighLevel, Google Workspace, and custom API integrations when there's no off-the-shelf connection.
