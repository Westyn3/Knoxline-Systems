SHT J-01 · JobNimbus

Your JobNimbus, fully wired

Payroll from job data, automatic review requests, and live dashboards — everything JobNimbus knows, put to work.

JobNimbus already holds everything a roofing company needs to run on numbers — every job, payment, rep, and status change. The problem is what happens next: commissions cross-referenced by hand, payroll eating hours every cycle, review requests that depend on someone remembering, and reports that mean exporting to a spreadsheet.

Knoxline builds on the JobNimbus API so the data works overtime. Payroll and commissions calculate themselves from job data — including split commissions, bonuses, and weekly guarantees. Review requests fire automatically when a job closes. And a live dashboard shows contracted jobs, cash collected, cost per acquisition, and rep-level performance across every location — updating on its own, no exports. Pair it with automated reporting and your whole back office runs from the CRM your crew already uses.

The gap

Where JobNimbus stops and the office work starts

JobNimbus is very good at what it was built for: moving a roofing job from lead to contract to production to paid. The trouble starts after the job closes, when someone has to turn that data into paychecks, reports and decisions. In most roofing companies we talk to, that someone is the owner, and the tool is a spreadsheet.

  • Payroll and commissions. The rules (split deals, tiered bonuses, weekly guarantees, commission on collected payments instead of contract value) live in one person's head and get rebuilt by hand every pay period.
  • Reporting. Questions like "what did each rep's leads actually cost us?" or "how many leads did we get per week this year versus last?" mean exporting to CSV and an evening of pivot tables.
  • Multiple locations. Comparing locations means filtering, exporting and stitching numbers together, so it rarely happens.
  • Reviews. Review requests go out when someone remembers, which means they mostly don't.

None of that is a JobNimbus problem. The data is already there. It just isn't connected to anything that does the work for you.

The build

What we build on top of JobNimbus

  • Payroll & commission engine

    Every job and payment syncs into a structured database. Commissions, splits, bonuses and guarantees calculate from that data using your exact rules. The owner reviews and approves payroll instead of building it.

  • Live owner dashboard

    Contracted jobs, cash collected, cost per acquisition and rep-level performance for every location, updated automatically and readable from a phone.

  • Lead & sales analytics

    Every lead on your board, counted by day, week, month and year, going back as far as your account does. Rep close rates, insurance vs. retail mix, and which lead sources are worth paying for.

  • Reviews & owner reports

    Review requests fire automatically when a job reaches the right status, and a monthly owner-ready PDF report lands in your inbox without anyone building it.

The process

How a JobNimbus build works

  1. Map your account

    We start with a 30-minute call, then walk through your JobNimbus setup together: statuses, job types, workflows and every commission rule, written down in plain English before anything gets built.

  2. Connect & sync

    Using an API key from your account, jobs, contacts and payments sync automatically into a database in your own Google Workspace. No exports, no copy-paste.

  3. Build & check against real pay periods

    Payroll, dashboards and reports are built on your rules, then checked against pay periods you've already run so you can see the numbers match before you rely on them.

  4. Go live & keep it current

    Once it's running, new reps, new locations and changed commission plans are updates, not rebuilds. On a retainer, we monitor the sync and handle those changes for you.

Common questions

JobNimbus integration FAQ

Do we have to switch CRMs or change how our crew uses JobNimbus?

No. Everything is built on top of the JobNimbus account you already have. Your reps and office keep entering jobs, statuses and payments exactly the way they do today. The automations read that data; they don't replace your workflow.

What access do you need to our JobNimbus account?

An API key generated by an admin in your JobNimbus settings, plus a Google account for the reporting side. We never need your personal login, and you can revoke the key at any time.

Where does our data live, and who owns it?

The synced data, payroll calculations and dashboards live in your own Google Workspace. You own all of it. If you ever stop working with us, the sheets, reports and history stay with you.

Can it handle complicated commission plans?

Yes. That is usually the reason owners call. We have built payroll around split commissions between reps, tiered bonus structures, weekly guarantees that reconcile against actual earnings, and commissions paid on collected payments rather than contract value. If you can explain the rule, it can be automated.

We run more than one location. Does that work?

Yes. Greater Roofing Solutions runs two Alabama locations on one system: shared data, with each location's jobs, revenue and payroll kept separate wherever it matters, plus a combined view for the owner.

What does a JobNimbus build cost?

It depends on what you need. A single piece, like payroll automation or a reporting dashboard, is a one-off project with a fixed quote agreed before any work starts. Owners who want the whole system built, monitored and adjusted as they grow go on a monthly retainer. A 30-minute call is enough to scope it.

Do you work with roofing companies outside Tennessee?

Yes. Knoxline is based in Knoxville, but JobNimbus work is done remotely, and our roofing client is in Alabama. We work with home-service companies across the Southeast.
