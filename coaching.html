The gap

The two numbers that run a coaching business

A coaching business or gym lives on how many clients come in and how long they stay. Most owners can't see either clearly, and growth makes the admin work worse.

  • Onboarding by hand. Welcome emails, forms and setup steps typed out for every new client.
  • Coach payroll in a spreadsheet. Sessions and payouts rebuilt by hand every pay period.
  • Churn as a feeling. Clients leave, but nobody can say when, why or which ones are at risk.
  • Growth by word of mouth only. No website that books consults and no reviews compounding your reputation.

The data to fix all of it is usually already in your membership software.

Common questions

Coaching & gym systems FAQ

Do we need to use Wodify?

No. Our deepest work is on Wodify, but the same approach works with other membership and scheduling software that has an API or reliable exports.

What does a retention study show?

When clients leave, how long they stayed, and the patterns before they cancelled, so churn becomes something you can measure and work on.

Will automation make our coaching feel impersonal?

No. Automation handles the admin (emails, forms, payroll math) so coaches spend more time coaching.

Can you build our website too?

Yes. We build fast, hand-coded websites with local SEO and Google Business Profile management for gyms, studios and coaching businesses.

What does it cost?

Single builds are fixed-quote projects. Ongoing systems work runs on a monthly retainer. We scope it on a 30-minute call.
