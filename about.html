Who's behind it

Westyn Hilliard

Knoxline Systems is run by a working data scientist, not an agency — and by someone who grew up in the industry it serves. Westyn comes from a line of home-service business owners. That's where his love for business, and for growing one, took root — and where he learned what ownership really feels like: wanting to grow your company but being stuck working in the back end of it instead.

He earned his bachelor's in business management while working full time at a financial institution, then his master's in data science. Today he builds production AI and analytics systems for one of the largest logistics companies in the world — using numbers and data to save the organization millions.

Knoxline brings that same engineering to businesses that pour concrete, fix appliances, and put on roofs. That combination is the whole point: home-service businesses rarely get access to someone who can build real data pipelines and automation — and data people rarely understand how a roofing company actually makes money. Knoxline sits exactly in the middle.

Every client works directly with Westyn. No account managers, no offshore handoffs, no ticket queues.
