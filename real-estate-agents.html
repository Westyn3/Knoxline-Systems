The exclusivity model

Why we only work with one agent per market

Real estate SEO is a zero-sum game at the city level. When we build a hyper-optimized website for an agent in Alpharetta — community pages, IDX search, metadata tuned to the searches buyers in that market make — that work competes directly against every other agent in Alpharetta trying to rank for the same terms.

So we made a choice: we go all in on one agent per market. That agent gets our full positioning effort. And we never take a second agent in any city where we already have a partner.

The practical result: the metadata we build, the community pages we write, and the Google Business Profile we optimize are working exclusively for one person in your market. The SEO value doesn't dilute because there's nobody on our side pulling in the other direction.

Common questions

Before you reach out

What IDX systems do you work with?

GAMLS for Georgia, TNMLS for Tennessee, and Placester as a fallback. If you're on a different system, tell me which one and we'll discuss the integration path. The goal is always the same: your search, your leads, nothing leaking out.

What counts as "my market" for exclusivity?

Your primary city or zip code cluster — wherever the majority of your listings and buyer relationships are. If you work Alpharetta primarily, we won't take another Alpharetta agent. Adjacent markets get discussed case by case.

I already have a website. Can you work with it?

Depends on the platform. Template sites (Wix, Squarespace, Placester, kvCORE) have structural ceilings that prevent the 100/100 score and the community-page architecture. It's usually faster and cleaner to rebuild. If you're custom-built, we'll audit it first.

How long until I see search results?

The site and GBP are live within 2–3 weeks. Local real estate is competitive, so honest ranking timelines run 60–90 days for community pages to gain traction, and 4–6 months for consistent inbound. The review system starts compounding from day one.

Is real estate a Knoxline specialty?

It's not the core — Knoxline is primarily built around home-service businesses. But two agent partners perform well, referral demand followed, and the SEO fundamentals are identical. I'm selective about agents I take on, which is part of why exclusivity fits here.
