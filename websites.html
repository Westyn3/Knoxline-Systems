The gap

Why most contractor websites don't bring in calls

For a home-service business, the website and the Google Business Profile decide who gets the call. Most contractor sites are built from the same page-builder templates, and it shows in the rankings.

  • Slow pages. Template sites load heavy scripts most visitors never use, and slow sites lose rankings and mobile visitors.
  • One page for everything. A single "Services" page can't rank for each job you do. Customers search for the specific service, not your company.
  • No local signal. No schema markup, no service-area structure and no connection to the Google Business Profile, so Google can't tell where you work.
  • Nobody maintaining it. The site launches, then sits. No Search Console, no updates, no idea whether it's working.

A website alone doesn't win "near me" searches. The website, the Business Profile and your reviews rank as a team.

Common questions

Website design FAQ

Why hand-coded instead of WordPress or Wix?

Hand-coded static sites have nothing to slow them down and very little that can break or be hacked. That means faster pages, better Core Web Vitals and less maintenance.

Is hosting included?

Yes. Hosting, maintenance and edits are included. You don't need a separate hosting plan or a developer on call.

Can I make changes myself?

Most owners would rather text a change and have it done that week. If you want to post updates yourself, we can add a simple editor for the blog.

Do you handle the Google Business Profile too?

Yes. Setup or cleanup, categories, services, photos and a review system. The profile and the website work as a pair, so we manage both.

How long until we show up on Google?

It depends on your market and competition. Search Console usually shows pages being picked up within weeks. Steady organic calls build over months as reviews and pages accumulate.

Do you build websites outside Knoxville?

Yes. We build for home-service businesses and real estate agents across the Southeast. ALLCON and our realtor clients are in North Georgia.
