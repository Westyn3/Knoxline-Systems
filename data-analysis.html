SHT D-01 · Data

Decisions on numbers, not gut feel

Financial analysis and forecasting for main-street businesses: where clients churn, which jobs actually make money, and what next quarter should look like — answered from your real numbers, not gut feel.

Fortune 500 companies employ teams of analysts to answer questions like: which customers are about to leave? Which jobs actually make money after labor? What should next quarter look like? Main-street businesses have the same questions — and their data usually holds the answers.

Knoxline is run by a working data scientist, and this is the service where that shows most. We take your real records — sales, clients, jobs, costs — and produce analysis you can act on: retention studies that show exactly where and why clients leave, forecasts grounded in your actual trend lines, and margin audits that reveal which work deserves more of your calendar. When those answers need to stay visible instead of living in a one-time study, we turn them into live dashboards and automated reporting.

The gap

The questions your data can already answer

Large companies pay analyst teams to answer questions small businesses face every day. The data is usually already there; nobody has time to dig into it.

  • Who is about to leave?. Churn shows up in the numbers before the cancellation does.
  • Which work actually makes money?. Revenue isn't profit. Some job types quietly lose money after labor and materials.
  • What will next quarter look like?. A forecast based on real trends beats a gut feeling.
