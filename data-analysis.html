SHT D-01 · Data

Decisions on numbers, not gut feel

Data-science-grade analysis for main-street businesses: where clients churn, which jobs make money, and what to do about it.

Fortune 500 companies employ teams of analysts to answer questions like: which customers are about to leave? Which jobs actually make money after labor? What should next quarter look like? Main-street businesses have the same questions — and their data usually holds the answers.

Knoxline is run by a working data scientist, and this is the service where that shows most. We take your real records — sales, clients, jobs, costs — and produce analysis you can act on: retention studies that show exactly where and why clients leave, forecasts grounded in your actual trend lines, and margin audits that reveal which work deserves more of your calendar.
