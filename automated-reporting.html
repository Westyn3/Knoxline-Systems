SHT R-01 · Reporting

Your numbers, without the spreadsheet nights

Your CRM already holds the answers. We pipe them into live dashboards and owner-ready reports that update themselves.

Every job, payment, and rep stat your business produces is already sitting in your CRM — the problem is getting it out. Knoxline builds automated pipelines from systems like JobNimbus and Housecall Pro into live dashboards: this month's contracted jobs, cash collected, performance by location, rep leaderboards, cost per acquisition, twelve-month trends.

The dashboard updates itself on a schedule — no exports, no copy-paste, no waiting on anyone. For the numbers you want pushed to you, we add automated PDF snapshots delivered weekly or monthly, written in plain English so you can read them between jobs. For companies that also need payroll, review requests, or recurring office workflows handled from the same CRM data, our business automation systems build on the same foundation.

What's included
  • CRM-to-dashboard pipelines (JobNimbus, Housecall Pro & more)
  • Live company & location-level dashboards
  • Rep and crew performance tracking
  • Cost-per-job & CAC reporting
  • Automated weekly / monthly PDF snapshots

The gap

Your CRM has the data. Getting answers is the hard part.

Every job, payment and rep stat is already in your CRM. But when the owner wants a straight answer, it usually means an export, a spreadsheet and an evening.

  • Built-in reports only go so far. They show the pipeline, but not cost per acquisition, rep profitability or year-over-year trends.
  • Spreadsheet nights. Monthly numbers get rebuilt by hand, so they're late and nobody fully trusts them.
  • No single view. Locations, crews and service lines each live in their own report.
  • Nobody looks until it's a problem. If the numbers aren't pushed to the owner, they don't get checked.

A live dashboard fixes that once, and keeps it fixed.

The build

What a reporting build includes

  • Automatic data sync

    Jobs, payments, leads and reps pulled from JobNimbus, Housecall Pro or your other systems on a schedule, with no exports.

  • Live dashboards

    Contracted jobs, cash collected, cost per acquisition, rep leaderboards and twelve-month trends, by location and company-wide.

  • Lead analytics

    Leads by day, week, month and year, going back as far as your data does, so seasonality and growth are visible.

  • Owner PDF reports

    Weekly or monthly snapshots in plain English, delivered to your inbox automatically.

Common questions

Automated reporting FAQ

Which CRMs do you work with?

JobNimbus and Housecall Pro most often, plus Wodify, Shopify and other platforms with an API.

Do we need special software to view the dashboard?

No. Dashboards live in Google Workspace, so you open them in a browser or on your phone.

How often does the data update?

On a schedule that fits your business, typically nightly. No one has to trigger it.

Who owns the data and dashboards?

You do. Everything lives in your own Google account.

What does it cost?

A dashboard is usually a fixed-quote project. Keeping it monitored and updated can be part of a monthly retainer.
