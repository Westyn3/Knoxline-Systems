The gap

The back-office problem the trucks can't fix

Roofing companies win or lose on how fast they respond during storm season and how clean the numbers are after. When volume spikes, the office is where it breaks first.

  • Commissions and payroll. Split deals, insurance vs. retail rules, bonuses and guarantees rebuilt by hand every pay period, usually by the owner.
  • Rep performance. Close rates, cost per lead and cash collected by rep are hard to see without hours of exporting.
  • Multiple locations. Each location runs its own numbers, so the owner never gets one clear view.
  • Looking like everyone else online. Template websites and thin Google Business Profiles that blend in with every storm chaser in town.

Most of that is already sitting in JobNimbus. The fix is connecting it to systems that do the work automatically.

Common questions

Roofing company FAQ

Do we have to use JobNimbus?

Our deepest roofing work is built on JobNimbus, but the website, Google Business Profile, reviews and Google Ads work doesn't depend on your CRM. For other CRMs, we look at the API on a call.

Can you handle insurance and retail jobs differently?

Yes. Insurance, retail and repair work can be tracked, reported and paid on separately, with their own commission rules.

We have more than one location. Can each have its own numbers?

Yes. Greater Roofing Solutions runs two Alabama locations on one system, with each location kept separate and a combined view for the owner.

How do automatic review requests work?

When a job reaches a status you choose, such as paid or completed, the customer gets a review request automatically. Nobody has to remember.

What does it cost?

Single builds, like payroll or a dashboard, are fixed-quote projects. Full systems run on a monthly retainer. We scope it on a 30-minute call.
